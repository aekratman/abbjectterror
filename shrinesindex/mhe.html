Maybe Happy Ending is a musical. It is, in fact, the musical of all time, forever.

It follows Oliver and Claire, two Helperbots in the distant future of Seoul, South Korea. Oliver is a sturdy but aging Model 3; Claire is a Model 5, made to be pretty and never to last. They are both desperately lonely after being retired to the Helperbot Yards.

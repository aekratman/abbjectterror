characters

olive

originated by: lyris schulman

affiliation: the bureau of artificial intelligence, godfrey

bio: Olive is a one-year old AI in the body of a sixteen year old girl. Her body was originally inhabited by a girl named Abigail, who is heavily implied to have commited suicide. Her major "role" is to integrate amongst humans in order to prove her worth as a product of the government.

character traits: Olive is an excitable person; she wants very badly to make friends. She teases Godfrey about as often as she covers for him when he is rude, and is protective of him. She is interested in dancing and enjoys sweets.

other notes: my favorite !!

godfrey

originated by: ephraim birney

affiliation: the bureau of artificial intelligence, olive

bio: Godfrey is a one-year old AI in the body of a sixteen year old boy. His body's orignal inhabitant is unknown. According to Ephraim Birney, Godfrey's mind originally belonged to the man who created the AI project; he's been remade and reset so many times that he remembers none of that. His major "role" is protecting Olive as they attempt to integrate into a human high school.

character traits: Godfrey is a blunt and serious person who cares quite deeply about Olive, his only friend. He wants very badly to make his Fatherparents proud, but even moreso is focused on protecting Olive. He has a very silly personality after you get past all that trauma. He also enjoys music and wishes to play the drums.

other notes: my other favorite!!

jessica

originated by: kirsten harvey

affiliation: kit

bio: Jessica is the junior student body president of Hurtz High School, and is selected as the student body representative for Olive and Godfrey's integration in the school. She struggles with her confidence and is in a relationship with Kit. when Godfrey outs Kit as having a relationship with Danny, Jessica is hurt, but later decides her friendship with him is more important. She is the first human student to refuse to allow the documentary to be put out in order to attempt to save Olive and Godfrey from discontinuation.

character traits: Jessica presents herself as a peppy, kind girl; however, she has a real capacity for cruelty and is in fact the only human student besides Emma to make fun of the AI students about possibly being killed. Throughout the show, she learns to trust in herself more. She has a lot of pressure placed on her by her parents and wishes to go to Harvard.

other notes: i love her so much !!

kit

originated by: john malonis

affiliation: none

bio: Kit is a Varsity basketball player at Hurtz High School. He is focused on getting into college on scholarship. He is in a relationship with Jessica, but after his older brother comes out as a gay man, he becomes curious about "other ways to be". Thus, he goes on a gay online site and asks Danny to send him some form of unspecified "pics". Danny outs him to Godfrey, and Godfrey outs Kit to Jessica after Jessica hurts Olive. Kit tries to beat Godfrey's scrawny ass to kingdom come, and slaps Jessica / pushes Danny in a rage when they try to stop him. Despite this, Kit is shown multiple times to be a caring and thoughtful friend, helping Jessica when she's nervous and trying to help Godfrey with his trouble socializing.

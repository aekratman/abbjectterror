I wrote the wiki for it and am working on migrating it to a different site that is not fandom.

The show means the entire world to me! I run a variety of fan content for it, and I'm fortunate enough to have made some incredible friends through it.

I am very attached to all the characters, especially Jonathan & Brandon.

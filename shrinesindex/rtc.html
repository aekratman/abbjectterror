this is my shrine for the musical "ride the cyclone", written by jacob richmond and brooke maxwell. it is the second installment in their uranium series and follows the six saints of saint cassian high school's chamber choir after their deaths at the fall faire of uranium city.

i've been listening to the show for a long time now and i love it dearly!
